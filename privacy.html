Privacy Policy

Top Dog Studios LLC · Last updated August 14, 2026

Top Dog Studios LLC ("we," "us") operates internal automated monitoring and notification systems, including an SMS alert program that sends operational system notifications to our own personnel. Personnel opt in via the form at our opt-in page.

Information we collect

For the SMS alert program, we collect only the mobile phone numbers of our own principals and employees, provided directly by each individual for the purpose of receiving operational alerts.

How we use it

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver operational system alerts and account notifications (for example, system health warnings, process halt notifications, and settlement or reconciliation notices). We send no marketing or promotional messages.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or any personal information with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties. Information may be processed by our messaging service provider (Twilio) solely to deliver the messages described above.

Opting out

Recipients may opt out of SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to Top Dog Studios LLC, New York, NY.
