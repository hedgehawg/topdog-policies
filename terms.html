Program description

The Top Dog Studios LLC operational alerts program sends automated system notifications by SMS to the company's own principals and employees who have opted in. Messages include system health warnings, process halt notifications, and settlement or reconciliation notices. This is an internal program; no marketing content is sent and no external consumers are messaged.

Opt-in

Personnel opt in by completing the web form at https://hedgehawg.github.io/topdog-policies/optin.html, where they enter their name and mobile number and check a separate, optional consent checkbox. Consent is voluntary and is not a condition of employment, system access, purchase, or any transaction.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with system activity. Recipients typically receive a small number of messages per week, with more during operational incidents.

Fees

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's mobile carrier plan.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to cancel and stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact Top Dog Studios LLC directly.

Support

Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages. For support with this program, contact Top Dog Studios LLC, New York, NY — email scott@palladiumtrain.com.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
